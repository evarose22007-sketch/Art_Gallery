The Great Wave off Kanagawa

Artist

Katsushika Hokusai

Year

1831

Description

The Great Wave off Kanagawa is one of Japan's most famous artworks. Created by Katsushika Hokusai, it shows a towering wave crashing over fishing boats with Mount Fuji in the background. It is celebrated for its striking composition and lasting influence on art around the world.
