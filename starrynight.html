The Starry Night

Artist

Vincent van Gogh

Year

1889

Description

The Starry Night is one of Vincent van Gogh's most celebrated paintings. It portrays a peaceful village beneath a swirling night sky filled with bright stars and a glowing crescent moon. The artwork reflects Van Gogh's emotional expression and unique artistic style.
