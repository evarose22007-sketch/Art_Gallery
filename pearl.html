Girl with a Pearl Earring

Artist

Johannes Vermeer

Year

1665

Description

Girl with a Pearl Earring is one of Johannes Vermeer's most celebrated paintings. Often called the "Mona Lisa of the North," it is admired for the subject's captivating gaze, delicate lighting, and the striking pearl earring that gives the artwork its name.
